ABOUT BACO

✦

Baco is a municipality in Oriental Mindoro where rural life and natural landscapes invite a more grounded way to travel. Explore Baco is a college-project concept for Filipino travelers, students, barkadas, and nature lovers looking for an approachable escape from Manila or Batangas.

Our mission is to imagine affordable, community-based trips that prioritize local guides, small-group experiences, and care for natural spaces. Our vision is tourism that supports livelihoods while encouraging visitors to leave places better than they found them.

The appeal is simple: combine river time, scenic outings, and everyday local culture in one flexible itinerary. Rather than promising exclusive access or fixed conditions, this guide helps travelers ask informed questions and plan responsibly.

HOW TO GET THERE FROM MANILA

  1. 01 / Take a bus or van to Batangas Port. Estimated travel time: 2–4 hours, depending on traffic.
  2. 02 / Take a passenger ferry to Calapan. Estimated crossing: 1–3 hours; verify schedules and sea conditions.
  3. 03 / Continue by van or other available local transport toward Baco. Estimated journey: 30–90 minutes, depending on your stop and connections.

All durations are planning estimates, not guarantees. Confirm routes, fares, operating hours, and pickup points with current operators.

GO WELL, TRAVEL LIGHT

TRAVEL GUIDE

Best time to visit

Consider a clear-weather day and start outdoor activities early. Check current forecasts and local access rather than relying on a fixed season.

Weather & water

Tropical showers can arrive quickly. Avoid rivers, falls, and exposed trails during or after heavy rain; heed local advisories.

What to bring

Carry drinking water, sun protection, a reusable bottle, a small first-aid kit, cash, and a waterproof pouch.

Dress for the day

Wear comfortable breathable layers and grippy footwear. Bring a light rain layer and a change of clothes for water activities.

Safety & local customs

Travel with an appropriate local guide, share your route, ask permission before photographing people, and respect private property and community spaces.

Protect the environment

Take litter home, stay on agreed paths, avoid disturbing wildlife, and follow any site-specific rules or visitor limits.

Conditions, schedules, prices, and access can change. Confirm everything locally before travel.
